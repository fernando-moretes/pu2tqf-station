Hello from Brazil! I'm Fernando, Papa Uniform Two Tango Quebec Foxtrot. Welcome to my station in São Bernardo do Campo, in the state of São Paulo.

By profession, I'm a solutions architect. Software engineering, cloud infrastructure, and cybersecurity are part of my world. I enjoy connecting ideas through Python, automation, and practical experiments. Amateur radio brings that same curiosity into the air.

My station is small, but every signal opens a door. On H F, I use a compact u S D X transceiver with roughly three to five watts of radio frequency output. An outdoor antenna and digital modes help me explore what low power can achieve. My first F T eight contact was with Chile, on November seventh, twenty twenty-six.

Behind the radio, a Raspberry Pi and my home laboratory help with audio, logging, and monitoring. I like building dashboards that turn signals into stories. A reception report is not the same as a completed contact, and I enjoy learning what the data really tells us.

There is much more to hear. With software defined radio, I explore aircraft signals, weather balloons, F M station information, and satellite reception. Some experiments work, others become useful notes for the next attempt. Following a tiny weather transmitter through the atmosphere is a wonderful reminder of how much is happening above us.

I also enjoy local V H F and U H F communication, and digital voice through a hotspot and the BrandMeister network. That worldwide connection uses the internet alongside radio. Different technologies, different paths, and plenty to learn.

Away from the microphone, you may find me learning Morse code, experimenting with LoRa and Meshtastic, working with E S P thirty-two boards, or designing a three D printed enclosure. My workshop is a meeting point for radio, software, and hands-on curiosity.

I share what I learn through my projects, station notes, and the Escuta Aberta educational radio listening project. If we meet on the bands, I would love to exchange a contact and hear your story. Thanks for visiting my station. Seventy-three from Fernando, Papa Uniform Two Tango Quebec Foxtrot!
